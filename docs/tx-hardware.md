# TX — Layer 6: ammunition, magazines, firearm type, vehicles

As of Nov 8, 2026. Statutes read on statutes.capitol.texas.gov on Nov 8, 2026 (Penal Code ch. 46 in full through § 46.14, §§ 42.01 and 42.12; Labor Code §§ 52.061–52.064), and S.B. 1596 (89th Leg., 2025) read on capitol.texas.gov. LEOSA covers carrying the gun concealed and the ammunition in it (§ 926C(e)); it does not cover magazines, the gun's lawfulness to own, storage, or weapons other than firearms (Layer 1 in `../../STATE_WORKFLOW.md`).

The answer: Texas hardware law is light, and it binds a retiree the same way it binds anyone else. There is no magazine limit, no hollow-point ban, no assault-weapon ban or roster, no purchase permit and no registration, and cities and counties may not add any (Local Gov't Code §§ 229.001, 236.002). What can still get a retiree charged: armor-piercing handgun ammunition, a handgun in plain view that is not holstered, carrying while intoxicated, a loaded gun a child can reach, and clubs or long knives in a § 46.03 place. The § 46.15(a)(5) certificate lifts only §§ 46.02 and 46.03; it does not touch §§ 46.05, 46.13, 42.01 or 42.12.

Groups are defined in `tx-definitions.md` (`txcert`, `retired`, `txltc`).

## What the app should say (for `rules_tx.js`)

- Hardware card: "Texas has no magazine limit, no hollow-point ban and no gun registration. It does ban armor-piercing handgun ammunition (Penal Code § 46.05), and a handgun that shows must be in a holster (§ 46.02(a-5))."
- Vehicle card: "You may carry loaded in your own car. If the gun is in plain view it must be holstered (§ 46.02(a-1)). Texas sets no storage rule for a parked car, but a loaded gun a child can reach is an offense (§ 46.13). Near a school, federal school-zone law has its own rules (see the school card)."
- Less-lethal card: "LEOSA covers firearms only. A baton or a knife over 5.5 inches is barred in every § 46.03 place unless you hold the Texas certificate (§ 46.15(a)(5))."
- Never say a magazine, round or gun type is "allowed": say "Texas has no limit found as of [date]."
